## The problem

Medical transcriptions contain dense terminology and large amounts of unstructured information. Finding, organizing and understanding that information can be time-consuming for doctors and medical students.

The project started as an NLP experiment for processing medical transcriptions and is now being expanded into a full-stack application designed around real healthcare and medical-learning workflows.

<WhatBroke>
  Medical language is significantly harder to work with than ordinary text. Terminology, abbreviations and clinical context make seemingly simple NLP tasks more nuanced.

  The current project is therefore being treated as a foundation rather than a finished product.

  **Lesson:** building for a domain means understanding the workflow, not just the model.
</WhatBroke>
