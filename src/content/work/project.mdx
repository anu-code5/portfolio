## The problem

My study group read the same papers but kept notes in five different places. Comments in shared PDFs were slow, flat and lost the context of where on the page they belonged.

Goal: everyone's highlights and margin notes on the same page, in real time, without anyone installing anything.

<MarginNote>margin note: started as a weekend hack before a [Course] exam.</MarginNote>
