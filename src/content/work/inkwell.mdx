## The problem

I could recite how Raft works for an exam but couldn't explain why each rule exists. The fastest way to find out was to build it, break it, and write down what happened.

Goal: a linearizable key-value store that survives node crashes and network partitions, with tests that prove it.

<WhatBroke>
  Under a specific partition, an old leader kept serving reads after a new one was elected. I traced it to a missing term check on the read path, added lease-based reads, and wrote a test that reproduces the bug in seconds.

  **Lesson:** "it passed the tests" only means as much as the tests do.
</WhatBroke>
